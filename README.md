# D-Mart Sales Analysis

D-Mart Sales Analysis is a portfolio project that applies Jupyter Notebook, Pandas, Numpy, Matplotlib, Seaborn and Power BI to analyze the Sample Superstore dataset D-Mart 
The goal is to convert raw sales data into clear business insights that support better decision-making around sales performance, profitability, product mix, customer segments, and regional trends.
This Power BI dashboard analyzes a D-Mart retail store's performance from 2023 to 2025 across five product categories and three customer segments, tracking key metrics including profit, sales revenue, order volume, unit prices, average transaction values, MoM, and YoY growth.

# Problem Statement
Retail businesses often collect transactional data but struggle to translate it into actionable insights. This project addresses that gap by organizing the dataset, cleaning the data, and analyzing sales patterns to answer practical business questions such as:

Which products and categories drive the most value?
Which regions and customer segments perform best?
How do sales and profit change over time?
Where are the biggest opportunities for improvement?

# Objectives
Understand the structure and quality of the sales dataset.  
Clean and prepare the data for analysis.  
Create useful time-based features for trend analysis.  
Perform exploratory data analysis on sales, profit, customer, product, and regional performance.  
Prepare the dataset for dashboard development in Power BI.
